## Narration

Oxygen makes up about a fifth of the air, and plants release it through photosynthesis. It is also the most common element in Earth's crust, locked inside rocks and minerals. Cooled enough, oxygen becomes a pale blue liquid. Hospitals give pure oxygen to patients who need help breathing. Industry blows oxygen through molten iron to make steel, and mixes it with fuel gas for cutting and welding. And liquid oxygen helps rockets burn their fuel in space.

## Accuracy notes

Check the generated footage against these before publishing:

- Oxygen gas is colorless; only liquid and solid oxygen are pale blue.
- Oxygen does not burn by itself; it helps other things burn. Avoid showing "burning oxygen".
- Air is about 21% oxygen.
