## Narration

Mercury is the only metal that is liquid at room temperature. It is so dense that a steel ball floats on it. For centuries it filled thermometers and barometers, because it expands evenly and is easy to read. A tiny amount of mercury vapor also makes fluorescent lamps glow. But mercury and its vapor are toxic, so countries agreed to phase out many of its uses. Beautiful, but handle with care.

## Accuracy notes

Check the generated footage against these before publishing:

- Never show anyone touching mercury with bare hands or handling it without protection.
- The phase-out refers to the Minamata Convention on Mercury (adopted 2013).
- Gallium and caesium melt just above room temperature; mercury is the only metal that is liquid at standard room temperature.
