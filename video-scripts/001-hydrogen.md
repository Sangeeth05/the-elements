- **Scenes:** 6

## Narration

Hydrogen is the simplest element of all: just one proton and one electron. It is the most common element in the universe, and stars shine by fusing hydrogen into helium. On Earth, most hydrogen is used to make ammonia for fertilizers, and to remove sulfur from fuels at oil refineries. In a fuel cell, hydrogen combines with oxygen from the air to make electricity, and the main by-product is water. Burned with liquid oxygen, liquid hydrogen has even carried rockets into space. The smallest atom, with a giant role.

## Accuracy notes

Check the generated footage against these before publishing:

- Hydrogen gas is colorless; the pink-purple glow only appears when electricity passes through it in a discharge tube.
- Do not show hydrogen balloons or airships as a current everyday use.
- Fuel cells produce water at the point of use; avoid claiming hydrogen is always "zero-emission", since that depends on how it is made.
