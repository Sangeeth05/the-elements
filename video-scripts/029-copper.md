## Narration

Copper is one of the first metals humans ever used, thousands of years ago. Mixed with tin it makes bronze, a metal so important that an entire age of history is named after it. Copper carries electricity very well, second only to silver, so it is the standard metal for wiring. It also forms water pipes and the coils of motors and transformers. Over time, weather gives copper a green coating, like the one on the Statue of Liberty. Ancient metal, modern world.

## Accuracy notes

Check the generated footage against these before publishing:

- The Statue of Liberty's skin is copper; its green color is a natural patina, not paint.
- Bronze is copper plus tin; brass is copper plus zinc.
- Avoid exact dates for the first use of copper; "thousands of years ago" is safe.
