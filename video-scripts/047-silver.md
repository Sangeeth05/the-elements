- **Length:** 34 seconds, 72 words of narration
- **Scenes:** 6

## Narration

Silver conducts electricity and heat better than any other metal. People have treasured it for jewelry, coins and tableware for thousands of years. Today, much of it goes into electronics and solar panels, where its high conductivity matters most. It reflects visible light better than any other metal, so it is used in mirrors. Over time silver tarnishes black, as it reacts with sulfur compounds in the air. Bright, beautiful, and useful.

## Accuracy notes

Check the generated footage against these before publishing:

- Tarnish on silver is mainly silver sulfide, from sulfur compounds in air, not rust from oxygen.
- Many modern mirrors use aluminium; say silver is "used in mirrors", not that all mirrors are silver.
